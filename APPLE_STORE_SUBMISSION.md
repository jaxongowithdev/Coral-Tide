## App Description

### Full Description

Aquarium Master is a beautiful aquarium simulation game where you catch fish, build your aquarium, and create the perfect underwater world!

Features:
- 52 Unique Fish Species - Collect them all across 4 habitats
- Animated Swimming - Watch your fish move naturally
- Strategic Feeding - Choose the right food for optimal growth
- Beautiful Decorations - Customize your aquarium
- Exploration Zones - Discover fish in Coral Reef, Deep Ocean, Ice Sea, and Hidden Cave
- Collection Album - Track your progress
- Tank Upgrades - Expand your aquarium capacity
- Coin Economy - Strategic resource management

Explore different zones, catch rare fish, feed them to help them grow, and build the most beautiful aquarium!
